# Inbox — Design direction

## Product surface

Inbox is a local contact intake prototype. It lets a visitor compose a message and an operator inspect, mark, copy, or remove the stored submission.

## Visual world

A correspondence room: mint paper, dark green ink, coral routing marks, a letter-like compose sheet, and an open-letter preview. It treats contact as a human handoff, not a fake CRM.

## Direction seed

Impeccable direction seed `a8597030`, operate mode, assigned direction 3. The grounded interpretation is a correspondence intake with a compose sheet and local inbox.

## System

- Palette: mint `#dfe9df`, paper `#fbfaf4`, deep green `#183530`, coral `#bb6049`.
- Typography: Libre Franklin for readable copy and Inconsolata for routing labels, dates, and states.
- Composition: two-column compose/inbox workspace; the selected message opens as a dark letter rather than a generic modal.
- Interaction: required form submit, local inbox, new/read state, filtering, selecting, copying, and removing.
- Responsive: the letter desk stacks below the compose sheet and preserves the inbox row hierarchy.

## Honesty constraints

Submission is explicitly local. No outbound email, CRM sync, or sender verification is represented.
